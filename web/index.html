Use deskport without Safari's bars

Safari can't hide its address bar and toolbar for a web page. A Home Screen icon opens deskport on its own, using the whole screen.

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar. It can be under the ⋯ button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open deskport from the new icon. The first time, it asks you to sign in again.
